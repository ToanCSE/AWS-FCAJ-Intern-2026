Event 1: FCAJ Meetup - Career Journey, DevOps & Corporate Culture

  • Event Name: FCAJ Meetup – Career Journey, DevOps & Corporate Culture
  • Event Date: June 13, 2026
  • Role: Student Participant

Introduction

The FCAJ Meetup was one of the technical sharing sessions organized as part of the AWS First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program for students interested in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence. The event brought together engineers and professionals from industry to share practical experiences in DevOps, Data Analytics, Cloud Computing, and career development.

Through the presentations and discussions, participants had the opportunity to gain insights into real-world engineering practices, understand workplace expectations, and learn about the technical and soft skills required to prepare for future careers in the technology industry.

Event Objectives

The meetup was organized to help students:

  • Gain a better understanding of the modern workplace and corporate culture.
  • Learn about current trends in Data Analytics, DevOps, and Cloud Computing.
  • Build a clearer learning roadmap and career development plan.
  • Explore AWS community programs available for students.

Speakers

  • Mr. Cường Nguyễn – Process Engineer
  • Mr. Đạt Phạm – Data Analytics Engineer
  • Mr. Trong H. Trương – DevOps Engineer
  • Danh Hoàng Hiếu Nghị – AI Engineer & AWS Community Builder
  • Đinh Trung Kiên – Lead Developer at Startup
  • Nguyễn Minh Thọ – Student Presenter

1. Career Journey & Corporate Culture

Speakers: Mr. Cường Nguyễn & Mr. Đạt Phạm

The speakers shared practical experiences from their roles as Process Engineer and Data Analytics Engineer, emphasizing how data supports business decision-making, process optimization, and problem solving within organizations.

Besides technical expertise, they highlighted several important soft skills valued by employers, including critical thinking, communication, teamwork, and data storytelling. They also introduced a professional growth model consisting of five stages: Follower → Learner → Problem Solver → System Thinker → Super Star, providing students with a clearer picture of long-term career development.

Another memorable topic was the corporate culture adopted by many international companies, such as the No-Blame Post-Mortem approach and an inclusive working environment, which encourage continuous learning and collaboration instead of assigning blame when incidents occur.

2. What Does a DevOps Engineer Really Do?

Speaker: Mr. Trong H. Trương – DevOps Engineer

This session provided a comprehensive overview of the responsibilities of a DevOps Engineer. DevOps is not simply about deploying systems or using technologies such as Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD. Instead, it focuses on bridging development and operations teams through automation, improving software quality, and accelerating software delivery.

The speaker also introduced a recommended learning roadmap for beginners, starting with foundational knowledge such as Linux, Networking, Git, and Python or Golang, before moving on to Docker, Containers, CI/CD pipelines, and Cloud Computing technologies.

In addition, the speaker emphasized that while AI can assist with software development and automation, engineers still need a solid understanding of system fundamentals in order to make appropriate architectural and operational decisions.

3. From First Cloud AI Journey to AWS Partner

Speaker: Danh Hoàng Hiếu Nghị – AI Engineer & AWS Community Builder

The speaker shared his personal journey from participating in the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program as a student to becoming an AWS Community Builder and working professionally in AI and Cloud Computing.

Throughout the presentation, students were encouraged to continuously learn, develop personal projects, and strengthen their technical skills. The session also introduced several AWS community programs such as the AWS Student Builder and AWS Community Builder, which provide opportunities for learning, networking, and participating in workshops and technical events.

The speaker also stressed the importance of building a strong portfolio, contributing to the technology community, and showcasing practical projects, all of which can significantly improve internship and career opportunities.

4. A Scalable URL Shortening Service on AWS

Speakers: Đinh Trung Kiên – Lead Developer at Startup & Nguyễn Minh Thọ – Student

The presentation demonstrated how to design a highly scalable URL shortening service on AWS. The speakers explained the overall workflow, from generating short URLs and storing mapping information to redirecting users to the original destination.

The proposed architecture utilized multiple AWS services, including Amazon CloudFront, AWS WAF, AWS Amplify, Amazon ECS, Amazon RDS, and Amazon ElastiCache, with each service playing a specific role in improving performance, scalability, and system reliability.

The session also highlighted the importance of caching strategies for reducing response times, minimizing infrastructure costs, and improving user experience.

From this presentation, I gained a better understanding of distributed system design on AWS and learned how different AWS services can be combined to build cloud-native applications capable of handling real-world workloads.

Outcomes & Key Takeaways

1. Better Understanding of the Professional Workplace

Through the speakers’ experiences, I gained a clearer understanding of the expectations within the IT industry. Besides technical knowledge, companies also value communication skills, teamwork, critical thinking, and problem-solving abilities.

2. Strengthened Knowledge of DevOps and Cloud Computing

The meetup helped me better understand the role of DevOps Engineers, an effective learning roadmap, and how AWS services work together to build scalable systems. These insights provided clearer direction for my learning journey.

3. Broader Career Perspective

The presentations about AWS Community Builder and the FCAJ program highlighted the importance of participating in technology communities, developing personal portfolios, and gaining practical experience through real-world projects. These are valuable factors in preparing for internships and future careers.

Applying the Knowledge to My Learning Journey

After attending the FCAJ Meetup, I realized that many of the ideas presented could be directly applied to my studies and future career development.

  • Continue strengthening fundamental knowledge in Linux, Networking, Git, and programming before learning advanced technologies such as Docker, CI/CD, and AWS services.
  • Build more personal projects to improve practical skills while creating a stronger technical portfolio.
  • Improve communication, teamwork, and presentation skills through academic projects and collaborative activities.
  • Participate in AWS community programs and technical workshops to expand knowledge and build professional connections.
  • Develop a problem-solving mindset and gradually adopt DevOps practices throughout the software development lifecycle.

Event Experience

The FCAJ Meetup was a valuable experience because every presentation was delivered by engineers with real industry experience. Instead of focusing only on theoretical concepts, the speakers shared practical knowledge, current technologies, and the essential skills students should develop before entering the professional workplace.

What impressed me the most was that each presentation was closely connected to real-world scenarios, ranging from business data analytics and DevOps workflows to the architecture of scalable cloud systems on AWS. These examples helped me better understand how the concepts learned at university can be applied in actual software projects.

The event also provided opportunities to interact with both industry professionals and fellow students who share similar interests in Cloud Computing. These conversations motivated me to continue learning AWS, participate in community activities, and better prepare myself for future internships and career opportunities.

Lessons Learned

After participating in the FCAJ Meetup, I learned several important lessons:

  • Strong fundamental knowledge is essential before learning advanced technologies.
  • Communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills are just as important as technical expertise.
  • DevOps is more than a collection of tools; it is a mindset focused on automation and continuous improvement.
  • Participating in technology communities helps expand knowledge, build professional networks, and create new career opportunities.
  • Continuously learning, building real projects, and keeping up with emerging technologies are key to becoming industry-ready.

Conclusion

The FCAJ Meetup was a highly valuable event for students pursuing careers in Information Technology. It not only introduced practical knowledge about Data Analytics, DevOps, and Cloud Computing, but also provided valuable insights into corporate culture, career development, and the professional skills expected in today’s technology industry.

Through the experiences shared by the speakers, I became more motivated to continue learning, develop personal projects, actively participate in the AWS community, and gradually build my career in Cloud Computing. Overall, the event was a meaningful experience that better prepared me for future internships and professional opportunities.
