## Event 2: Gameday - 8 Teams Battle

- **Event Name:** Gameday - 8 Teams Battle
- **Event Date:** 20/06/2026
- **Role in the Event:** Student Participant

---

## Introduction

**Gameday - 8 Teams Battle** was a knowledge competition organized as part of the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program. Unlike workshops that mainly focus on knowledge sharing from speakers, Gameday was designed as a direct team competition, creating an engaging learning environment where participants could review learned knowledge while improving their problem-solving skills, quick thinking, and teamwork abilities.

The competition included **8 teams**, with each team consisting of multiple members collaborating to solve questions related to AWS Cloud. The teams were paired into direct elimination matches, creating a competitive but friendly atmosphere focused on learning and interaction. This event was not only an opportunity to evaluate technical knowledge but also a chance for participants to exchange experiences and strengthen teamwork.

---

## Objectives of the Event

The event was organized to provide an academic playground for FCAJ participants, helping them reinforce their AWS Cloud knowledge through an interactive competition format rather than traditional theoretical learning.

The main objectives included:

- Reviewing and strengthening fundamental knowledge of AWS Cloud.
- Improving problem-solving skills and quick decision-making under time constraints.
- Developing teamwork, communication, and collaboration skills among team members.
- Creating opportunities for participants to connect, exchange experiences, and learn from each other.
- Encouraging students to continue exploring and practicing Cloud technologies.

---

## Main Activities & Contents

### 1. Opening Session

The program started with an introduction to the competition rules and scoring system. The organizers then divided the **8 teams** into **4 competing pairs** following a direct elimination format. Each team had a short preparation period to discuss strategies, assign responsibilities, and coordinate before starting the competition.

The atmosphere became more exciting as teams prepared for their matches and followed the results. Although the competition was highly competitive, participants maintained a friendly spirit and supported each other throughout the event.

---

### 2. AWS Cloud Knowledge Competition

Each team participated in matches containing questions related to the knowledge covered throughout the FCAJ program. The topics included popular AWS services such as **Amazon EC2**, **Amazon S3**, **Amazon RDS**, **Amazon VPC**, **IAM**, and **AWS Lambda**, along with concepts related to Cloud Computing, security, system deployment models, and practical scenarios.

Each question required team members to quickly discuss, analyze, and agree on the final answer within a limited time. Besides testing theoretical knowledge, many questions required participants to apply their understanding to select suitable AWS services for specific real-world problems. This made the competition more engaging and practical.

---

### 3. Determining Finalist Teams

After completing all matches, the organizers summarized the results and announced the **two winning teams** that advanced to the final round. The remaining teams ended their competition journey but stayed to observe, interact, and support the finalist teams.

Due to the limited duration of the event, the final round was not held on the same day and was scheduled for another session according to the organizers' plan. Before the event ended, the results were announced, participants took commemorative photos, and teams discussed interesting questions from the competition.

---

### 4. Program Summary

At the end of the event, the organizers shared feedback about the teams' performance and summarized the highlights of the competition. Some questions were explained in more detail to help participants better understand the concepts and improve their knowledge gaps.

Although the final round had not taken place yet, Gameday still created a positive learning atmosphere and motivated teams to continue preparing for the next stage of the competition.
